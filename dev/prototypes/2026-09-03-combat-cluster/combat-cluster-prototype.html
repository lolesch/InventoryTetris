What the headless sweeps already show

40–200 seeded Runs per cell, this file's model (node sweep.mjs):

  1. The packed archetype picks the build. Raw sustain at S5: vs a Brute pack physical lasts ~2× longer than magical (big single-target Strikes chew the few high-HP bodies); vs a Skirmisher pack magical lasts ~1.5× longer than physical (the 3-target Cast clears the swarm before it piles on). It's count vs per-target HP — AoE counters numerosity, single-target counters bulk. Not the naive "AoE for the pack of tanks" read.
  2. Hybrid owns nothing and loses nothing badly. Second in both specialist packs (~75–85% of the winner), roughly on par on genuinely mixed composition, and it's the only build that clears mixed without a death-rate. Three-way viable in the weak sense (all progress); two-way in the strong sense (only physical & magical are ever the best pick). A discovered lean — not a blocker, per #18.
  3. Fixed Roster + endless Encounters holds under two enemies. Thornwood (S2 Brute-pack): every build farms it, Recalled:Bag at ~4 Encounters, full HP. Ashfall (S5 Skirm-pack) at ×1 gear: Recalled:HP or Death — a wall. Gear is still the ladder: physical crosses ashfall from "dies at ~5 Encounters" to "farms to the cap" between ×1.15 and ×1.3 (a one-shot breakpoint on Skirmisher HP), magical opens it more gradually.
  4. XP-on-clear gives the Encounter boundary teeth. A Run driven off mid-Encounter forfeits that Encounter's whole pot — ~400 XP at ashfall, about half an Encounter. And it defuses the auto-focus worry: the lowest-HP Strike still eats Skirmishers first (ttk ~1–3 s vs Brutes' 4–9 s), but with no per-kill XP that's just sensible threat triage, not an exploit.
  5. CastThreshold is still a texture knob. Sweeping 0.05→0.9 with two enemies moves XP/min <1% until the very top. ADR-0010's "pack-deleting burst" doesn't pay for itself against a continuous spawn. Keep the slider, soften the framing.
  6. The bag is still the whole "return to Town" pressure. Turn it off and a capable build farms a fixed-difficulty Location forever. Bag capacity + loot-filter defaults are loop-load-bearing.
