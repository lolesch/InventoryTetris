Guided walkthroughs

What the batch sweeps already show

Run headless over 150–200 seeds per cell (this file's model, node sweep.mjs):

  • Encounter scale → finite Roster. With finiteRoster:false the Encounter counter never moves — there is no clear, no beat, no next. The Roster is the only thing that makes an Encounter a unit.
  • Run scale → endless Encounters, no cap, no rescale. A realistic Run always ends on a Recall trigger or Death: on Easy the bag fills at ~5 Encounters (~80 s); on Hard the HP line trips at ~1–2. An encounterCap of 8 fires in <1% of Easy runs — the bag beats it every time. A hard cap is dead config.
  • Fixed difficulty holds. Every build sustains Easy forever (900 s cap, full HP). No build sustains Hard at base gear — all die by ~80 s. Gear is the ladder: magical crosses from "dies at 105 s / 6 kills" to "farms 480 kills" between ×1.25 and ×1.4. That cliff is the "geared hero outgrows the Location" rule — it needs no restart-rescale.
  • All three builds are viable — each clears Easy indefinitely and clears a gear-dependent slice of Hard. Lean: magical ~1.5× the XP/min of physical (better AoE farm) but dies soonest raw; physical lasts longest raw; hybrid sits between with no dominance either way. Not a two-way choice.
  • CastThreshold is a texture knob, not a power knob. Sweeping 0.05→0.95 moves XP/min <3% — a saved burst that deletes a Pack 2 s early just pulls the next Pack 2 s closer. The "pack-deleting burst" in ADR-0010 does not pay for itself on a continuous spawn.
  • Engagement is a real knob, and Packs punch through it (ADR-0010 intent): physical at Engagement 1 lasts 141 s vs 45 s at Engagement 5; a spawnBatch [4,4] overshoots any Engagement setting and spikes incoming.
